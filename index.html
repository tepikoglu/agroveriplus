FAQ

Tough questions, honest answers

Can AgroVeri+ detect fake certificates?

Phase 1: integrity. Phase 2: source verification via ECOCERT/IMO + OTBIS. Phase 3: AI format analysis.

What about garbage in, garbage out?

Phase 2 solves this — certification bodies upload directly, preventing fake documents from entering.

How does IPFS hashing work?

Cryptographic hash per document. One pixel change = different hash. Distributed and permanent.

Is AgroVeri+ EUDR-compliant?

Yes — plot-level geolocation, traceability, and DDS preparation for Dec 2026 / June 2027 deadlines.
